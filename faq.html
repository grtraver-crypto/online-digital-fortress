Frequently asked questions

The questions founders ask before they act.

Who is this book for?

It is written for founders, creators, and operators who already have something valuable to protect and who want a more disciplined structure for growth.

Is this legal advice?

No. The book and website are educational resources designed to help you think more clearly about structure, risk, and preservation. They are not a substitute for legal advice.

What is the Digital Fortress Audit?

The audit is a free resource designed to help you assess the structure of your business and identify the places where separation, control, and protection may be missing.

What makes this different from typical business advice?

This framework focuses on defensibility, ownership boundaries, and long-term resilience rather than only growth, visibility, or short-term momentum.
