Why My Wyoming LLC Got Flagged as a “Foreign Business” in Tennessee

The real story behind why this framework exists — and what it taught me about what “protection” actually requires.

After forming a Wyoming LLC — on paper, one of the strongest asset-protection jurisdictions in the country — I was charged by the State of Tennessee to register it there as a “foreign business.” I had no building, no location, and no employees in Tennessee. It was standard advice, technically correct, and it made no intuitive sense at all. That discovery is what sent me down the research path that became the Triple Shield Architecture — and this post tells that story in full.
