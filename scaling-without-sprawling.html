Scaling Without Sprawling

Growth that respects the structure — and the difference between scaling a business and just making it bigger and more exposed.

Growth is supposed to be the goal, but growth is also exactly what breaks a structure that wasn’t built to hold it — new team members hired into the wrong entity, new products launched without updating the licensing agreements, new revenue lines that quietly recreate the single-LLC problem you already solved. This post covers how to map growth to the right entity, delegate without losing containment, and tell the difference between scaling and simply sprawling.
