The Three Money Streams That Should Never Cross

Revenue, management fees, and royalties — the documented, one-directional flows that keep three LLCs from becoming legally one.

Three separate LLCs only protect you if the money between them moves in a documented, consistent, one-directional pattern: revenue into Creator, a management fee out to Management, a royalty out to Holding. The moment those lines blur — an unpaid invoice here, a personal expense covered from the wrong account there — you’ve started building the paper trail a court uses to decide your “three companies” were never really separate at all. Commingling is the single most common way a well-designed structure quietly fails.
