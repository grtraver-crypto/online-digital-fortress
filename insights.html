Chapter 2 · The Framework Read Now

The Triple Shield Architecture, Explained

The Triple Shield Architecture is the framework the rest of this book builds on: three separate LLCs, each with exactly one job. Creator faces the market and takes the risk. Management runs contractors, vendors, and systems without ever owning anything valuable. Holding stays passive, owns the intellectual property and the equity in the other two, and never touches a customer. It sounds simple because it is — the discipline is in keeping each layer doing only its own job, so a single dispute anywhere in the business can never reach everything you’ve built.
